Google have released a heap of videos from their internal archives:

> In addition to helping distribute content from across the world, Google would like to share videos featuring our company.

Some great stuff in there, especially the ever-brilliant [Seth Godin doing a presentation](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=-6909078385965257294&q=seth+godin) that seems to cover the central themes of a selection of his books in under 50 minutes. I saw Seth present years ago when he was at Yahoo! and he’s always stuck in my mind as one of the most natural and compelling presenters out there.
