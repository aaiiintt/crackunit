I was attempting to write something about Friday’s Microsoft Day that I went to. But I visited [Live Local](http://local.live.com/) search to look something up, then realised I could see my own house from the air, from multiple different angles. And it’s, dare I say it, way slicker than Google maps in terms of visual quality. Not sure if it’s the same everywhere, but in central London it’s very very cool!

Give it a go.
