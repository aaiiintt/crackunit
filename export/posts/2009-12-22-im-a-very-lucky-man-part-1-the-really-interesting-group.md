I met with [Ben](http://noisydecentgraphics.typepad.com/) yesterday morning and he gave me a lovely gift. A set of DataDecs – Christmas decorations based on my social media data. I can almost hear a global murmer of youwhats. But it’s a really simple and sweet idea…

A lovely gift from the [Really Interesting Group](http://reallyinterestinggroup.com/)

Here’s the shape of my 4 decorations printed inside the card.

[![The Card - Showing My Stats](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2595/4206573088_b63e6ea317.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4206573088/ "The Card - Showing My Stats by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

The red decoration you can see at the top is my Last FM Scrobbling by month – seems like I’m getting up to speed again after a summer of not listening to so much music during babytime.

This blue decoration shows the apertures I’ve used when taking photos on Flickr:

[![Flickr Apertures](/wp-content/rescued/farm5.static.flickr.com/4045/4205814927_05f711252b.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4205814927/ "Flickr Apertures by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

This shows how many miles Dopplr says I’ve travelled over the last 12 months.

[![Dopplr Trips - Whoops!](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2546/4206573976_1eef56f1f5.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4206573976/ "Dopplr Trips - Whoops! by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Or rather it shows that I’ve not been using Dopplr this year (sorry Dopplr guys!).

And the size of this snowman’s head shows how many followers I have on Twitter…

[![Twitter Followers](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2644/4205815497_b5979ee471.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4205815497/ "Twitter Followers by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

And here you can see how the shape of my baubles compares with other people’s…

[![A Key - Showing Where I Sit](/wp-content/rescued/farm5.static.flickr.com/4037/4206573348_baa4530d1e.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4206573348/ "A Key - Showing Where I Sit by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

My snowman’s head is looking proportionally OK. And I’m certainly proud of my varied use of camera apertures!

Thank you [Really Interesting Group](http://reallyinterestinggroup.com/) – that’s a really interesting Christmas gift.
