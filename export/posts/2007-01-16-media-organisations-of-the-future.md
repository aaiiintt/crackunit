Faris has a good write up of a presentation by Simon Waldman of the Guardian: [What is a media company?](http://farisyakob.typepad.com/blog/2007/01/what_is_a_media.html)

I saw the same presentation (or a version of it) at a Microsoft day late last year. And I have to say I thought he put forward a really compelling case. I don’t want to rip-off Faris’ slides that he ripped-off from Simon ;-) So you’ll have to go [here to see them](http://farisyakob.typepad.com/blog/2007/01/what_is_a_media.html).

I do share Faris’ reservation that it works for people like the Guardian, or MTV, and bits of the BBC. But I’m not sure if it holds true for [De Agostini](http://www.deagostini.co.uk/) where their whole business model relies on transportation of physical stuff (binders, lord of the rings tiles, pony statuettes, etc.).

Interestingly, or not, De Agostini is one of the few things I’ve not manged to find in [Wikipedia](http://www.wikipedia.org/) recently).
