Just turned up at the [CAT](http://www.creativitycat.com) conference to find out that I can’t use ‘Presenter View’ in Keynote. This has happened before and I just winged it. But today I figured I could find a quick way to get everything onto my iPhone in a simple way that will work with any version of Keynote and doesn’t require any fancy apps…

So here’s what to do if you find yourself in a ‘can’t see my presentation notes’ kind of an emergency.

First, don’t panic…

Then hit ‘print’ in Keynote…

Your special friend is ‘save PDF to iPhoto’…

[![](https://i0.wp.com/posterous.com/getfile/files.posterous.com/crackunit/0CnhTu83chEJ0mbT06lyngnJrw4qauP3xHOZXC3FgFdWO3Dk03ZJMnAcyTxG/Keynote1.png.scaled.500.jpg?resize=500%2C390)](https://i0.wp.com/posterous.com/getfile/files.posterous.com/crackunit/9c6R1032fYXebixwD5AgA7QlhKB0prqJEPUT07xRwctoAmdXVphrwVK91QqQ/Keynote1.png.scaled.1000.jpg)

Then just go to iTunes and sync your photos…

[![](https://i0.wp.com/posterous.com/getfile/files.posterous.com/crackunit/D7F2vaSiG6ITs2P2Ix9IyubhRX1onzbV27w219BjFetvdzLyZoqk9gsKIt1c/iTunes1.png.scaled.500.jpg?resize=500%2C321)](https://i0.wp.com/posterous.com/getfile/files.posterous.com/crackunit/PyNVWqV1nRVP7aSz64uKu7TwZ43t6R5zggaVWILAl0A58BvnOm1dErGH0hbJ/iTunes1.png.scaled.1000.jpg)  
And you’re done.

You now have a perfectly usable set of notes inside the ‘Photos’ app on your iPhone.  
[![](https://i0.wp.com/posterous.com/getfile/files.posterous.com/crackunit/rwU1T8HgKpa3REZy4iYnT6iz0UqbGZRDqiOmAjI5Zv38LpCmfhpyHRQwyVY2/IMG_2447.jpeg.scaled.500.jpg?resize=500%2C375)](https://i0.wp.com/posterous.com/getfile/files.posterous.com/crackunit/2J2xlaVRfsPcrMb51BDVySGK8KbFGdaIVpO4gDDFmi0sRz5uI9vozG6Wayk6/IMG_2447.jpeg.scaled.1000.jpg)  
There might be a quicker way, but this felt like a pretty tight workflow in a sticky situation.
