Forget about Nu-Rave it’s just a passing fad. You want Neo-Rave. That’s the real business. Bang Face are having a 3 day Neo-Rave Armageddon special at Camber Sands Holiday Park.

They say: Musically you’ll get a Neo-Rave Explosion of Acid, Jungle, Rave Hardcore, Drum & Bass, Breakcore, Techno, Electronica, Dubstep, Electro and Abstract Dance. Plus they’ve got the first ever Human Rave Bomb on the Friday night.

What more could you possibly want. Ever.
