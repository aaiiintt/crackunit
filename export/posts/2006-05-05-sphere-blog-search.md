[Sphere: a useful new blog search engine](http://www.sphere.com). Seems to index things very fast. My posts from yesterday were already in there. It’s got a particularly nifty ‘custom date range’ function which shows time and volume of posts in a nice way.  
Designed by Adaptive Path who’ve posted an [account of how they arrived at the design they did](http://www.adaptivepath.com/publications/essays/archives/000614.php).

Thanks to [Leisa](http://www.disambiguity.com/) for the links, her [appraisal of the design](http://www.disambiguity.com/2006/05/sphere-review-hidden-treasures-20-orange-the-perils-of-style-for-design-agencies/#more-182) also makes for interesting reading.
