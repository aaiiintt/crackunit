![brighton](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/sunny-brighton.jpg)

I don’t know why, but I felt the urge again.

I went upstairs to the loft on Saturday and put myself to work making another mix. In a kind of similar vein to the last one ([Rainy Saturday in the Loft – A Mix](/2007/07/02/rainy-saturday-in-the-loft-a-mix/)) and I can’t quite tell if the weather made any difference. It was certainly a bit nicer to do with sun coming through the skylights.
