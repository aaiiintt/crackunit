You know you’re a bit of a geek when…

*…You chuckle to yourself at a URL.*

I was just visiting the T-Mobile site and got this error page. Fair enough it’s very early in the morning and they’re probably doing some kind of update so I’m not too upset really.

![tmobile](/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/tmobile.jpg)

Then I noticed the URL.

![t mobile url](/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/tmobile-url.gif)

Imagine how much I laughed when I noticed that the error pages were sitting in the *‘common’* directory. Surely that’s not the right message to be giving off. Ho Ho.

(If you don’t understand why that might be funny, keep it that way. You’ll be a better person for not being amused by this kind of thing).
