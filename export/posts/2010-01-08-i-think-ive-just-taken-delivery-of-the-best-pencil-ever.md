For a much better review and explanation as to why the world of pencils has just changed FOREVER check out [Dave’s Mechanical Pencils](http://davesmechanicalpencils.blogspot.com/2008/04/uni-kuru-toga.html) (the foremost authority on such things). And if you want to experience the future of mechanical pencil technology for yourself – you can [buy them at Amazon.co.uk](http://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/product/B002N5MBIY?ie=UTF8&tag=crackunitcom-21&linkCode=as2&camp=1634&creative=19450&creativeASIN=B002N5MBIY) for £5.60 – a tiny price to pay for such a marvel, surely?
