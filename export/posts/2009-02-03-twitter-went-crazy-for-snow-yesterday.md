In my personal bit of Twitter it felt like there was a lot of talk of snow, trains and snowmen. The thoroughly awesome [Twist](http://twist.flaptor.com) proves that it was a global trend. Why had no-one alerted me to [Twist](http://twist.flaptor.com) before? It’s fantastic and does all kinds of trending around Twitter. For some reason I couldn’t embed a live verison of the graph – [but here’s a link to it](http://twist.flaptor.com/trends?gram=snow%2C+train%2C+snowman&table=0&tz=0).

I was astonished to find that the Superbowl had around 8% of Tweets on Sunday at 1am!

![Trends for ](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20090203-cfsd2rbxxxyrh76hfm76md44d3.jpg?w=660)

But blow me! Snow had 11% of Tweets on Monday morning at 9am. God bless the Brits and our fantastic ability to talk about the weather. Makes you proud doesn’t it…

**Slightly Embarrassed Edit:** Of course it did snow elsewhere in the world and I’m not claiming that it was just BritTwit that caused the snowspike. Well pointed out James.

This [Twist](http://twist.flaptor.com/) thing could really take over my life. I must step away…
