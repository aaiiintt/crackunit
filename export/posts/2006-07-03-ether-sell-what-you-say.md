![Ether Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2006/07/D32_ether-logo1-med_V1.jpg)A new site. In ‘zeta’! (Via [TechCrunch](http://www.techcrunch.com)) Yoiks.

This sounds interesting. And a recipe for disaster if done badly.

Basically you set up your Ether number (a freephone number) through the site, and set your rate in $. Then when people call you for advice they’ve been setup and their card has been authorised. The longer you talk the more they pay.[Ether: Sell Therapy, Counseling, Tax Advice, Computer Help, and More – Sell what you say](http://www.ether.com/default.aspx)

A sample list of sellers [can be seen here](http://www.ether.com/CommunityServer/forums/thread/36292.aspx).

I’ll be surprised if it doesn’t degenerate into lots of smut talk lines very fast. And it doesn’t look like it’s got a user ratings thing either which it should have really. Otherwise how am I supposed to know if the person can be trusted or not.

But I still think selling your time online in this way is an interesting idea.
