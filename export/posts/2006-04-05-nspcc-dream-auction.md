[![NSPCC Dream Auction](/wp-content/uploads/2006/04/Picture%202.jpg)](http://www.dreamauctionfullstop.com)Here’s a little (in size) project Poke have done for the NSPCC. [The NSPCC Dream Auction](http://www.dreamauctionfullstop.com) is aiming to raise a huge amount of money to help to end cruelty to children. There’s a number of ways to get involved, from a huge gala auction at the Royal Albert Hall (that I’m sure I won’t get an invite to), 1000s of eBay lots (which I’m sure I can bid on) through to the sale of rather fetching mobile phone lanyards.
