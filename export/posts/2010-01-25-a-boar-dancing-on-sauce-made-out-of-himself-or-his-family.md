Picked up this postcard in Carluccios. I love the illustration and the type. But I always find it slightly odd when an animal looks so joyous about being made into food. Or then again maybe he’s waving that pan and spoon around in protest? it’s so hard to tell with the facial expression of boar. The illustrator is Christopher Brown, [his work can be seen here](http://www.centralillustration.com/cb/cb.php).
