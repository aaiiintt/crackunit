![Burger King Fark](/wp-content/thumb-bkpumpkin24ya_01.jpg)[Adrants covers BK getting ‘Farked’](http://www.adrants.com/2005/10/burger-king-gets-farked-sells-halloween.php) – an interesting story of a brand getting stacks of exposure though an online community running away with their ‘icon’ and co-opting it in their own way. The article kind of makes it sound as if BK had a hand in it.

I was chatting with Rob from [B3ta.com](http://www.b3ta.com) about this earlier and he spoke to Drew from [Fark](http://www.fark.com) who made it very clear that there was absolutely nothing commercial about the Farking at all.
