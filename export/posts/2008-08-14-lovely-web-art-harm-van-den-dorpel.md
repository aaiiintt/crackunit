[![White Rectangle - Harm van den Dorpel, 2008](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080813-nynbhkegki3q8febugj1d96s3y.jpg?w=660)](http://www.harmvandendorpel.com/)

From the beautiful [White Rectangle](http://www.harmvandendorpel.com/whiterectangle.htm) to the strangely captivating [Ping Pong](http://www.harmvandendorpel.com/pingpong.htm) this guy has got some really [lovely animations and web art in his portfolio](http://www.harmvandendorpel.com/). You won’t get it without seeing his styles of animation. You need to go and have a bit of a dig around his work and see it in motion: [Harm van den Dorpel](http://www.harmvandendorpel.com/)

I’m really in love with [Codec Breeze](http://www.harmvandendorpel.com/codecbreeze/index.htm) (a series of pieces that use a weird and subtle animated effect that I think has been generated just using different levels of compression in parts of the image).
