I [posted about the return of Boo.com a while ago](/2006/01/25/boocom/).

Being a connected company someone’s been out doing the rounds letting ‘interested’ people know that Boo are moving to the next phase of relaunch – [see this comment](/2006/01/25/boocom/#comment-4807). I don’t mind people doing this, and I don’t consider it to be comment spam.

[But have a look at the new Boo.com](http://www.boo.com). I did, and nearly wept. If the holding page is anything to go by, I’d swear that their business model must be based on advance sales of [BooHoo](http://www.amazon.co.uk/Boo-Hoo-Dot-Com-Story/dp/0099418371) vol 2.
