![](https://i0.wp.com/i92.photobucket.com/albums/l8/iaintait/westham.jpg?w=660)Newspaper graphic 2 of the weekend. This isn’t an infographic, it’s a visual gag from the back page of The Sun (or it could have been the Mirror). A great visual gag. I’m not sure if it’s been done before (I don’t follow football enough to know whether West Ham have been involved with the Russians before). What I liked about it was the fact it was just really subtle and they’d not tried to ram it down your throat too much.

If anyone’s got any other interesting graphics from this weekends papers (ideally involving a country in Eastern Europe in some way) I’d love to see them.
