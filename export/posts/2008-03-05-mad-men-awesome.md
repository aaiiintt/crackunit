Having said recently that I don’t watch much good TV. I ended up watching Mad Men on BBC4 on Sunday night thanks to a recommendation from mum.

Truly great.

![](/wp-content/image_well/mad_men2.jpg)

From the team that brung the world the Sopranos it’s a beautifully crafted drama about New York ad men in the 60s. It’s [available on BBC iPlayer for now](http://www.bbc.co.uk/iplayer/page/item/b009364t.shtml). I really recommend checking it out. I’d not heard about it at all, [but it appears to be the best new TV show of last year in the US](http://www.time.com/time/specials/2007/top10/article/0,30583,1686204_1686244_1691341,00.html) – just shows how unglobal I am.

The world is different now, but only a bit.
