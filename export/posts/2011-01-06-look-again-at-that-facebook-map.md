I did what I suspect a lot of people did. I saw this beautiful Facebook visualisation and went “oooh”. My oooh contained just a couple of things:

1.  Isn’t that pretty
2.  Facebook is big and global

But I just read [this account by Paul Butler](http://www.facebook.com/notes/facebook-engineering/visualizing-friendships/469716398919), the intern who made the thing. And all of a sudden it took on a whole new dimension. It gets a little technical in places, but you should read it, you’ll view the whole thing as more than just a pretty picture once you have. Go on [read it](http://www.facebook.com/notes/facebook-engineering/visualizing-friendships/469716398919).

All of a sudden you realise that there isn’t a background map here. Everything you see, including the recognisable shapes of countries is created by connections between people.

And once you’ve read Paul’s piece, [click and view the high-resolution version of the map](http://sphotos.ak.fbcdn.net/hphotos-ak-snc4/hs1382.snc4/163413_479288597199_9445547199_5658562_14158417_n.jpg) and drink it up.
