[![postal service](/wp-content/uploads/2006/01/postal_service.jpg)](http://www.postalservicemusic.net/)I like the [The Postal Service](http://www.postalservicemusic.net/) a lot. Their album is great. I also like Apple a lot. Their products are great. As has been [well documented](http://www.eliteproductionswi.com/apple-ps-more.htm) the latest Apple Intel Ad [looks a lot like the video](http://www.eliteproductionswi.com/apple-ps.htm) for “Such Great Heights” by the aforementioned band. Ben from The Postal Service expresses his disappointment on [their site.](http://www.postalservicemusic.net/) But, in a sweet twist of irony the video ends up topping the iTunes video chart.

Shows the power of a bit of controversy online. The digital equivalent of kids standing round a schoolyard fight. “Scrap! scrap! scrap!”.[
