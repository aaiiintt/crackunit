It’s either going up, or down, depending on which way you walk along Western Road in Brighton…

It starts (or ends) at £1.25 for a withdrawal. Then it’s £1.35:

[![Western Road Cash Machines](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3012/2877150968_0ec5843e40.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/2877150968/ "Western Road Cash Machines by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Then just a few metres along it’s £1.38! Or perhaps even more confusingly ‘up to £1.38’ (it’s actually just £1.38, I tried).

[![Western Road Cash Machines](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3244/2877150140_760628be06.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/2877150140/ "Western Road Cash Machines by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Just goes to show, it pays to shop around.
