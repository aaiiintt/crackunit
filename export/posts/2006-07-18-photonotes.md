[Nik’s](http://www.vi-r-us.com/) set up a group called ‘[Boast it Notes](http://www.flickr.com/groups/boastitnotes/)‘ on [Flickr](http://www.flickr.com). It’s simple really. I don’t think I should even stoop to the level of explaining it. It came about after he set up a group on Flickr called ‘Street Style’ now it’s got 63 members and photos in it. It was a hole waiting to be filled. Maybe ‘boast it notes’ will be the same. Probably not.

But the whole notion using photos as notes is an interesting one. i found myself doing something rather odd on Saturday. I was going to someone’s house, and I had their address on my computer. But instead of sending it to my phone using some nonsense technology. Or writing it down, as a non-geek with a pen might do. I photographed it on my phone.

[![](/wp-content/rescued/static.flickr.com/69/192635801_c96e36c163_m.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/44124263380@N01/192635801 "Note to Self")

This might seem odd. But it was quick. Free. And less likely to be lost than a scrap of paper. Or maybe I’m just in love with my new Sony Ericsson K800i.
