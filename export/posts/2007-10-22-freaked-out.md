I was walking back from a meeting when I saw these 3 things in quick succession. I was freaked out. First up…

![howards way](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/dsc00126.jpg)

[Howards Way](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howards'_Way) – I thought. Funny an 80s TV show street, ha ha. (Turns out the TV show is Howards’ Way, which makes a big difference, but at the time I didn’t know).

Then 20m later I saw:

![Max Headroom](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/dsc00127.jpg)

[Max Headroom](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Headroom_%28TV_series%29) another show from back in the day. I chuckled to myself.

Then I looked round and:

![West Wing](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/dsc00128.jpg)

The blinking [West Wing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_West_Wing).

All of a sudden I felt like I’d been transported into some odd parallel universe where my life is a bizarre Truman Show vs. TV Times mashup. Honestly all 3 within a minute of each other.
