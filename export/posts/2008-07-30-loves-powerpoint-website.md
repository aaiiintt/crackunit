![love](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080730-r7pcg7cu4n6xheiix1nf7hyn96.jpg?w=660)

I’m gutted. But impressed. And glad.

Love have made their new website in Powerpoint: [http://www.lovecreative.com/](http://www.lovecreative.com/)

I’m gutted because I’ve been whanging on about doing a website in PowerPoint for the last couple of years. But impressed because I couldn’t figure out how to do it, or to be arsed with the task of actually getting it done. And I’m glad it’s the Love guys who did it, [because i like them](/2008/01/29/loving-love/).

I’m especially loving their use of the standard PPT sound effects. Brilliant.

And thanks to [Jono for posting a comment about the site](/2008/07/21/making-art-with-microsoft-office/#comments), nice one.

**EDIT: By the way I’m not saying that I think it’s a good way to build a site, or even that I like the site as it is, I think it’s an intereresting thing though making a site in Powerpoint – it gives it talkability even if you don’t like it…**
