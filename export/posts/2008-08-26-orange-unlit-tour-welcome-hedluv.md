We’re putting on a tour with Orange. It’s part of the whole ‘I am’ thing. [The Unlit Tour](http://www.i-am-everyone.co.uk/orangeunlit/) is about an artist going on the road and creating his own ‘I am’ story as he goes. The tour is going to be ‘made by everyone’. From the people who open up their houses to act as gig venues and the bands who’ve offered to support to the strange and wonderful randoms that Jont and Dave meet on their travels.

This video basically tells the story better than I could. It’s the film of their first few days on the road.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/orz-sbuGFPM" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

My particular highlight is a band called [Hedluv](http://www.myspace.com/hedluv) who played in Penzance. They’re kind of like [The Mighty Boosh](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mighty_boosh) meeting [Goldie Lookin Chain](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldie_Lookin%27_Chain) in the keyboard section of Toys R Us.

To see them live watch about halfway through the video above.
