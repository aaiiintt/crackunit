I reckon that we’re going to see a lot of stealth in 2009.

The first example I’ve found of this emerging trend is the hiding of vegetables in Baked Beans. Heinz have managed to cram 2 of your 5 a day into just 1/2 a can of these ‘Hidden Veg Beans’. Sneaky, smart and just a little bit sad.
