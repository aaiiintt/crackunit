I’ve never seen it like this. There’s only 1 train in the station and everything is broken. But luckily (I think) I’m on that train…

Update: The train has gone a different way from normal. It went over a big viaduct which was very pretty. Now we’re being told that the driver has to switch ends of the train. So we have to let a small, average-looking, bald-headed man walk through the train… How intriguing…
