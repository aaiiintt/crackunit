[Flipping the Funnel (ebook)](http://sethgodin.typepad.com/seths_blog/2006/01/flipping_the_fu.html) – as ususal [Seth](http://sethgodin.typepad.com) writes in a way that makes you think, “duh, of course, who didn’t know that”, but then you realise that you didn’t, and he’s just implanted something new and brilliant in your brain.

A really straightforwardly written ebook about how tools like [del.icio.us](http://del.icio.us) and [flickr](http://www.flickr.com) can be used to give your company’s fans a voice that can have a really unique kind of cut through. Read it or weep.
