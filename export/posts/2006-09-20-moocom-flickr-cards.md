Great great great!

Get your Flickr photos printed onto mini-cards (they’re like 1/2 height business cards). They look a bit like They are v2.0 of [Pleasurecards](http://www.pleasurecards.com/) (thanks Stef) which I have some of and are great quality. The application which sucks your photos from Flickr and lets you edit your cards is really simple and well built.

And the best bit of all?

If you’ve got a Flickr pro account you **get 10 of the little blighters for free**.

[moo | we love to print](http://www.moo.com/)

Interesting looking company with some interesting people working at it and interesting backers… Interesting.
