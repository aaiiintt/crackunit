[![long tail on last fm](/wp-content/uploads/2006/04/Picture%2011.jpg)](http://www.last.fm/recommended/music "long tail on last fm")[Supersmart Ged](http://renaissancechambara.blogspot.com/) (who I know from Yahoo!) pointed me to the longtail recommendations engine on [last.fm](http://www.last.fm). I’d been to the page but I’d never noticed the slider: [have a play, it’s really good (you might need to be a user to access it).](http://www.last.fm/recommended/music) It’s a really good and simple way to demonstrate in real terms the notion of ‘long tail’ to someone who hasn’t ever seen it before.

And on the topic of all things with long tails, [Chris Anderson](http://www.thelongtail.com/)‘s book about it is on [pre-order at Amazon now](http://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/product/1401302378/202-0782173-6275057?n=283155).
