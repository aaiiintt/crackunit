I went to [Pride in Brighton](http://www.brightonpride.org/) on Saturday. And it was great. The weather was good and gays and straights were out in huge numbers having fun. Struck me how nice it was to have an event that was founded in people just wanting to celebrate being themselves. Maybe we should all do it from time to time.

The guy in the photo was dying to have his photo taken, so I thought he could be ***double famous*** by being featured on my blog ;-)
