This image of Jim Henson and Kermit – [posted here](http://www.youmightfindyourself.com/post/185561198) – has over 440 notes (at time of posting). That’s 440 people who’ve either re-blogged or ‘liked it’ on Tumblr. That’s a lot of kudos and viral spread going on. Mind you, it is a bloomin’ lovely picture.

Tumblr, I’m watching you. Like a hawk. A hawk with a lazy eye.
