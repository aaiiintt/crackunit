And on the subject of [Jordy van den Nieuwendijk](http://jordyvandennieuwendijk.nl/). I manged to trick him into giving me a 100% original and exclusive work.

I *pretended* that he should have a play with my Samsung Galaxy Note and ‘see how good the stylus is’ . (Knowing full well he wouldn’t be able to *not* do something good).

[![image](/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/wpid-nieuwendijk_4-01.jpeg "NIEUWENDIJK_4-01.jpeg")](/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/wpid-nieuwendijk_4-01.jpeg)

.

He’ll probably hate me for posting this (it was done on a bumpy bus ride after a couple of beers) but I think it’s pretty wonderful. Sorry [@JvdNieuwendijk](https://twitter.com/jvdnieuwendijk)

I wonder if I can trick any other artists into creating original works for me using the ‘try my stylus’ method…?
