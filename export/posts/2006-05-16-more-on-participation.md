![the 72:1.8 rule](/wp-content/uploads/2006/05/72rule.gif)Following on from the earlier post about people participating in communities I thought this post contained some great stats: [The 1% Rule: Charting citizen participation](http://customerevangelists.typepad.com/blog/2006/05/charting_wiki_p.html). Points to the fact that 1% of Yahoo! Group members start groups and 10% of visitors ‘synthesize’ (or interact) with that content. On Wikipedia 1.8% of users have submitted 72% of the content.

The 72:1.8 rule isn’t as catchy as the 80:20 rule. But at the same time when people start banging on about user generated content it’s always handy to have some numbers like this in the back pocket. [Read more.](http://customerevangelists.typepad.com/blog/2006/05/charting_wiki_p.html)
