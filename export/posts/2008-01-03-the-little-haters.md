I was feeling like the little haters had moved in, and I wasn’t going to be able to start blogging again. But then I just posted up some rubbish about cows with yellow teeth and bosh: I was back in the saddle and the haters were gone. I guess that’s the luxury of having your own blog rather than having to ‘create to order’ for other people – I’m finding it much more challenging to get rid of the little haters in a work context… I’m hoping it’ll come… Soon…
