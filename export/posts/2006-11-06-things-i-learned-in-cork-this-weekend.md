[![](/wp-content/rescued/static.flickr.com/105/290382404_9a67de6e3e_d.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/44124263380@N01/290382404 "Limericks and Blarney")

Limerick and Blarney are both real places in Ireland, and you go the same way from Cork to get there. I really wanted to go and kiss the Blarney stone, but apparently so does everyone else.

[![](/wp-content/rescued/static.flickr.com/111/290381900_485c993231_d.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/44124263380@N01/290381900 "Segways in Cork")

Everyone in Cork travels on Segway. All the people who are advertising the new 3 store do anyway. They still look like fun to me.

[![](/wp-content/rescued/static.flickr.com/113/290383163_bb86595838_d.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/44124263380@N01/290383163 "Chuppa Chupps Poster")

Some posters in Cork seem deeply inappropriate.

[![](/wp-content/rescued/static.flickr.com/105/290381558_31f7092053_d.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/44124263380@N01/290381558 "The Best Kebab Shop Name Ever")

They’ve got the best named Kebab shop in the world. Apart from perhaps Kebabylon.

[![](/wp-content/rescued/static.flickr.com/117/290380052_c5b54e5f93_d.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/44124263380@N01/290380052 "Vend-a-Scent")
