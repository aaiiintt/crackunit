My Nintendo Wii has been in a box for the last couple of months. But this weekend I had some mates over and we played it for a good few hours.

It really is a very good thing.

When glancing down my RSS feeds this morning (I think I was especially attuned to Wii news) I spotted these two absolutely blinding bits of info:

[Lego Star Wars: The Complete Saga](http://uk.wii.gamespy.com/wii/lego-star-wars-the-complete-saga-/802001p1.html) is coming out. With Wii LightSabre controls – woooh!

And [Beautiful Katamari](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_Katamari) is rumoured to be coming to Wii too – there’s a [video of the Xbox 360 version here](http://www.gamepro.com/microsoft/xbox360/games/previews/112049.shtml). It looks nuts!
