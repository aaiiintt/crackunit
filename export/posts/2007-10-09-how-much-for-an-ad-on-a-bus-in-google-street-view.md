If you’ve not seen it yet Street View in Google Maps is amazing. [Lifehacker reports that it’s now in 15 US cities](http://lifehacker.com/software/street-view/) (and it’s coming soon to the UK judging by the fact that we saw a Google cam car driving round London the other day).

![map](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/maps.jpg)

I’ve you’ve not mucked around with it, get on a map of NY or Chicago and have a play with Street View. It’s almost more impressive than Google Earth.

While I was playing I came across this:

![google\_coke\_zero.jpg](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/google_coke_zero.jpg)

And wondered what’s the value of a bus side inside Google maps? It’s lot more appealing to me than the Coke Zero ads in the UK ;-)
