I saw these when I was in the US earlier in the year. They’re pouffes made out of massive wool. You can [see them on the DWR website](http://www.dwr.com/product/flocks-pouf-medium.do). I liked them a lot, they look substantial yet cozy.

Today I stumbled across a picture of a woman making them. Now I love them.

![http://www.dwr.com/text/viewer.htm?id=12665](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20091220-djjwttgqawkcm5jnfqrw845eys.jpg?w=660)

Then as you do I stumbled across the website of the designer, Christien Meindertsma, and her awesome PIG 05049 project. Which then led me to her TEDxAmsterdam talk about the same project.
