Anyone know any brilliant technical director types who’d be up for working at Poke? Our Igor is leaving to go to a super job at the Guardian. Being an agency and doing work for ‘the man’ we can hardly persuade him against going to work for the leading voice of the liberals.

He will be sadly missed. But the ‘Gor must be replaced…

So if you know anyone who’d be able to lead and inspire one of the most brilliant teams of enthusiastic and talented developers I’ve ever come across, punt them over here. I don’t really know what I’m talking about when it comes to what they should know technically, but if they’d would be able to conceive of how to do something like the Orange Balloon Race and make it work, they’re the kind of person we might be looking for. Our technology set is most things *excluding* .net and the Microsoft side of life.

You’ll need to demonstrate having run quality teams of developers as well as being able to solve technical problems with style and flair.

We’re quite a way down the road with a couple of interesting looking people so get in touch sooner rather than later. Email me direct. You ought to be able to guess my email address: my name is iain, and my domain name is crackunit.com – if you join the two things together using the standard symbol used in such addresses you’ll be pretty close.
