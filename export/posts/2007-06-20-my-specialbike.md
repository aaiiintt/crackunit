I picked up my custom recycled bike from the guys at [Specialbike](http://www.specialbike.co.uk) last night. Unfortunately the gods decided to unleash one of the biggest thunderstorms in the universe immediately after picking it up so we got soaked. But I still love it!

![specialbike1](/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/img_1856.jpg)

It’s got hub gears, so the chain doesn’t bounce. And you can change gears while your standing still without that horrible crunchy thing you get with other bikes.

![specialbike2](/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/img_1857.jpg)

And it’s got a twist grip shifter to keep it all nice and neat.

![specialbike3](/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/img_1859.jpg)

And if you look at the frame you can see it’s history, just a bit. It’s got classic old school lugs and you can see the vintage pump holder along the top tube. Which I like.

![specialbike4](/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/img_1861.jpg)

Sophie got a very different, but equally awesome bike too…

![sophie’s specialbike](/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/img_1862.jpg)

Bikes are cool.
