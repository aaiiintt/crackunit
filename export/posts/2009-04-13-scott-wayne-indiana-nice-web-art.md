[Scott Wayne Indiana](http://www.39forks.com/) sent me a nice email and a couple of links to his sites. The first one is his photo a day project – [http://coinop.tumblr.com/](http://coinop.tumblr.com/). I really like them. That’s one of them above.

He also sent a link to 39 Forks which is a collection of his art projects. There’s some really nice stuff in there including the $200 car project where he bought a car for $200 and drove it till it broke. Here’s the road movie:

<object width="425" height="344"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/P261XnBRCd4&amp;hl=en&amp;fs=1"><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"></object>

And perhaps my favourite thing of all is [Web Street](http://www.39forks.com/WebStreet.htm)

[![Web Street on 39forks.com](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20090413-frtsg8y7si194te7j52un412jr.jpg?w=660)](http://www.39forks.com/WebStreet.htm)

Based on the insight that more street art is seen on the web than on actually on the street, he’s set up a blog of digitally manipulated street art. Things that have never actually existed on the street. But look like they might have done. [Check it out](http://www.39forks.com/WebStreet.htm).

Things like [Banksy for Livesavers](http://39webstreet.blogspot.com/2008/10/banksy-for-life-savers.html).

![zipBanksyForLifeSaver](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20090413-jp53kn4ny6nk9wmw3s1xr13mqk.jpg?w=660)

I’m always impressed by people who just get stuff done. They make me feel inadequate. In a good way. Have a [dig around](http://www.39forks.com/) and find some things.

Nice work Scott.
