[Vasco](http://www.vascoalvo.com/) my favourite Portuguese nutjob from Poke is putting on a night at [Cafe OTO](http://cafeoto.co.uk/) in Dalston. Which according to the Observer Food Monthly is the 4th coolest place to eat in Britain. And according to the Observer Food Monthly it’s the coolest place in Britain according to Italian Vogue. With that many ‘coolest according to’ accolades you need to go there at some point. Hell, even I need to go there at some point.

Anyway he’s bringing over 2 of his favourite guitarists from Portugal for one night only.

[Norberto Lobo](http://www.myspace.com/norbertolobo)

I love the fact that Vasco has dipped into his own pocket to put on a night of music that he loves. And he’s a nice guy so I’m getting behind it.
