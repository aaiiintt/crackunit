Some of the excellent geeks at Poke have hacked together / masterfully crafted [BakerTweet](http://www.bakertweet.com/). It’s a wonderful hardware / software solution for alerting people to the latest oven output from the Albion bakery.

It’s a wireless [Arduino](http://www.arduino.cc/) thing that can be customised via a web interface to allow for various custom messages to be Twittered at the twist of a dial and the push of a water/flour-proof button.

What’s brilliant is The Albion actually using it. And it actually works.

![Albion's Oven (AlbionsOven) on Twitter](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20090401-bdbpg9hd7nqbtm8k28eu84752a.jpg?w=660)

Fingers crossed they keep it up and they see a significant rise in flash sales.

If you live or work in the Redchurch Street / Shoreditch High Steet area you can now follow [@albionsoven](http://twitter.com/albionsoven/) on Twitter and be told when the freshest baked goods have dropped. But you’ll have to be quick I just heard they’ve had a run on buns.
