![Grazia Teabuddy](/wp-content/uploads/2006/11/grazia%20teabuddy.jpg)

[Teabuddy.com](http://www.teabuddy.com) (our social web app for managing tea-making and preferences) has come to the boil again. It’s one of those odd sleeper projects. We did it ages ago and at first it got picked up by [Wired](http://www.wired.com/news/culture/0,1284,67357,00.html), then a bit later by [The Guardian](http://lifeandhealth.guardian.co.uk/experts/richardjohnson/story/0,,1772722,00.html). Now it’s really entered the mainstream with a mention in Grazia magazine (it was the second item in their ‘cool chart’ last week).

That’s one of the things I love about online. Things can just live on forever, getting discovered by new audiences up to years after they originally launched. It’s something that clients are often uncomfortable with, they’re used to campaigns having a launch date and a finite lifespan. Those rules just don’t apply any more. I guess that’s the long tail of advertising.

Our other big sleeper project [Globalrichlist.com](http://www.globalrichlist.com) still has massive peaks and was mentioned in [Daily Candy](http://www.dailycandy.com/article.jsp?ArticleId=28064&city=4) this weekend too.

I don’t know if there’s a sudden upsurge in female publications getting more into online stuff, but its interesting that two of our old projects have been picked up by big Womens’ publications in recent weeks…
