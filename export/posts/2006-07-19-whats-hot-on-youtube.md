Really useful breakdown of the 100 most viewed clips on YouTube. Nice work: [No Man’s Blog: YouTube trends report #1](http://nomansblog.typepad.com/no_mans_blog/2006/07/youtube_trends_.html)

The top line is that of the top 100:

-   58 are user generated content
-   31 are music videos
-   4 are commercial adverts
-   3 are commercial virals
-   2 are movie trailers
-   2 are Asian tv show clips

There’s further breakdown of the user generated content [on the site](http://nomansblog.typepad.com/no_mans_blog/2006/07/youtube_trends_.html). But interesting to see that ‘real’ ads have been more viral than ‘viral’ ads. Goes back to the whole [viral is bad, viral is good (stuff)](/2006/06/07/i-hate-virals/) argument from before.
