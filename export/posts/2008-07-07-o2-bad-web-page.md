The new O2, we’re out of stock of the 3G iPhone, page is bad. For a number of reasons. The whole upgrade/ordering thing was a farce, but [more on that elsewhere](http://boagworld.com/usability/lessons_from_the_o2_failure/)…

But worst of all is the padlock in the top right hand corner.

![](/wp-content/uploads/image_well/iphone_3g.jpg)

In my book a padlock on web pages means something. Normally that the page is secure. The [page in question](http://upgrades.o2.co.uk/maintenance.html), is not secure:

![](/wp-content/uploads/image_well/iphone_tech.jpg)

A big company like O2 should know better than to mess about with the sanctity of the padlock.
