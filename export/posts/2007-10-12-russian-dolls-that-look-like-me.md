Just saw this thing about [Paint Your Own Russian Dolls on PSFK](http://www.psfk.com/2007/10/pic-paint-your-own-russian-dolls.html).

Now it’s unlike them to be so late to the game. [Weiden+Kennedy had a window display of painted dolls back in June ’06](http://wklondon.typepad.com/welcome_to_optimism/2006/05/more_dolls.html).

But *ages* before that. September ’05 in fact (for my birthday) Sophie had made me a set of Russian Dolls that look just like me. Isn’t she clever, and nice, and talented, and well ahead of the global trends curve.

And in a strange twist of fate she’s now working at Weiden+Kennedy. I don’t think it was the dolls that got her the job though…?
