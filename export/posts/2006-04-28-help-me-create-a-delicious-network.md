A new feature of [del.icio.us](http://del.icio.us/) is the ability to create [your own networks.](http://blog.del.icio.us/blog/2006/04/its_made_out_of.html) It’s a replacement for the slightly confusing inbox feature.

I’d love to create a little network of like-minded people so I can keep in touch with the hot stuff that other people are bookmarking. If you use del.icio.us and would like to ‘network up’ feel free to add me to your network. And it’d be great if you could [leave your ID in the blog comments](/2006/04/28/help-me-create-a-delicious-network/#respond). That way both me, and other readers, can hook-up in a lovely world of del.icio.us-ness.

My del.icio.us name is [iaintait](http://del.icio.us/iaintait) leave yours [in the comments](/2006/04/28/help-me-create-a-delicious-network/#respond).

Oh yeah, it might seem a bit unmanagable at first. But the smart thing to do is to subscribe to your network RSS feed and you can keep a track of what people are up to. (Oh, and don’t forget the newish [private saving](http://blog.del.icio.us/blog/2006/03/private_saving_.html) feature…a welcome addition for most normal people).
