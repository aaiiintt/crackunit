![](https://i0.wp.com/farm1.static.flickr.com/126/390000813_ce4ea1c3d3.jpg?resize=179%2C239)Funny article on ‘[How to turn your Flickr crush into real romance](http://blog.wired.com/wiredphotos41/2007/02/step_1_be_reali.html)‘, specially posted for Valentines day over at Wired. Starts off with a story of a real life Flickr Romance, then gives you the 10 steps to finding love on Flickr.

I particularly liked this bit on ‘[Inventing a Cuter You](http://blog.wired.com/wiredphotos41/2007/02/step_4_invent_a.html)‘:

> But mostly there should be  
> pictures of you with attractive women, you next to motorcycles, you lifting  
> heavy things, you looking artsy and blurry. Use those Photoshop skills to  
> project a lifestyle your crush would want to be a part of.

Dig beneath the humour and there’s some semi-insightful nuggets into how you can extract information and learn things about peoples’ characters from their posting habits.
