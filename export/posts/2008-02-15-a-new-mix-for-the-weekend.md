Actually that’s a lie. It’s not a ‘new’ mix. It’s a mix from last weekend. But I’m posting it now.

This is from [Asi’s](http://no-mans-blog.com/) birthday party last Friday. It was a party in a pub. So I felt I had to play a bit more ‘rowdy’ than I normally would to entertain the drunkards. Hence it’s a bit full on in places – and has some well-known stuff in there too. *Disclosure – I did tidy it up when I got home, but it really was almost perfect when I did it live, honest ;-)*

Download from here if you so desire: [http://www.drop.io/asi\_party](http://www.drop.io/asi_party) (click listen, then you can download the MP3 file)
