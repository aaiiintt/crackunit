Great list of the [Top 10 Best Presentations Ever](http://www.knowhr.com/blog/2006/08/21/top-10-best-presentations-ever/), a pretty tough list to compile and they’ve done a nice job. There’s a few in here that I’ve seen before, and a few that I haven’t.

For Apple geeks of particular note is [Steve Jobs launch of the first Mac in 1984](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=8631701936876784775), funny to see him presenting in a double breasted suit and bow tie.

As an aside I’m stunned almost daily at how much amazing video content is becoming freely available online. [Martin Luthor King’s “I have a dream” speech in full](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=1732754907698549493) is just one of those things. It’s starting to feel like pretty soon we’ll have access to a complete archive of the most interesting moments in film.
