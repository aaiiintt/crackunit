[I Love London](http://iaintait.vox.com/)

Actually I don’t. That’s the whole point. Me and London haven’t been getting along all that well recently.

So I thought I’d try a little experiement. To see if blogging about great things in London can inspire me to think more about great things in London. And make me more attuned to noticing these things around me.

Basically, ***can blogging inspire positive thought***.

The project was inspired by a conversation with [Russell](http://www.russelldavies.com) last week about how blogging can affect your view of the world (in a good way) and an invite to try out [Vox](http://www.vox.com) (a new service from [Six Apart](http://www.sixapart.com)). I started this morning.

Have a look: [http://iaintait.vox.com](http://iaintait.vox.com). Leave your thoughts.
