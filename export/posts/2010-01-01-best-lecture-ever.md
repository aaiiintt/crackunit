Inspiring, memorable *and* entertaining. Prof Barry Nalebuf’s (author of [Why Not?: How to Use Everyday Ingenuity to Solve Problems Big and Small](http://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/product/1422104346?ie=UTF8&tag=crackunitcom-21&linkCode=as2&camp=1634&creative=19450&creativeASIN=1422104346)![](http://www.assoc-amazon.co.uk/e/ir?t=crackunitcom-21&l=as2&o=2&a=1422104346)) lecture from Yale Economics class is truly great. It’s focused on innovation, creativity and problem solving. Particular highlights for me are the segments on upside-down food, and the use of the “What Would Donald Trump Do?” method. Both things I’ve recounted to various people on various occasions.

[Academic Earth](http://www.academicearth.org) is full of amazing lectures from some of the best schools and biggest brains around. The [Stamford course on Programming Methodologies](http://academicearth.org/courses/programming-methodology) is superb – I’ve only watched 4 of them so far, and I’m a bit scared of watching any more in case I become dangerous.
