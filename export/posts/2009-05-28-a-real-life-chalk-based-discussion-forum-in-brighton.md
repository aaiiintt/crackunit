Spotted this the other day. It’s a big chalk thing on the prom in Brighton ([click here for a full 6000px wide panorama](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3317/3572967368_47d8902f41_o.jpg) where you can see all the details). It invites people to leave gossip on the pavement. It’s massive. And they supply chalk.

I thought it was a bit like a discussion forum. Then as I looked at the detail the thought solidified before my very eyes…
