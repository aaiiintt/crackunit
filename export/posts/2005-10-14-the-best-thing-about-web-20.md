[Creating Passionate Users: The best thing about Web 2.0](http://headrush.typepad.com/creating_passionate_users/2005/10/the_best_thing_.html) – As per normal Kathy Sierra makes some amazingly valid points over on one of my favourite blogs.

Isn’t it bugging when you read articles that express what you’re thinking so much better than you ever could?

Another case in point with Kathy is her article on [‘Dignity is Deadly’](http://headrush.typepad.com/creating_passionate_users/2005/09/dignity_is_dead.html). She talks about the painful puberty that companies face when they try to grow beyond their startup roots. And questions the need to grow-up in the first place. A question dear to my heart (professionally and privately;))
