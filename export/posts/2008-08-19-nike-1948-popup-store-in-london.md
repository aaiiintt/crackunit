## Edit: Popup store has popped-down.This store now appears to be closed. So don’t make a special trip to see it.

If you’re in the Shoreditch area there’s a nice temporary pop-up Nike store just opposite Market Sports on Bateman’s Row.

It’s open:

[![Nike 1948 Popup Store in London](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2004/2777192323_4b43eef47d.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/2777192323/ "Nike 1948 Popup Store in London by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

There’s a big concrete AirMax shoe outside:

[![Nike 1948 Popup Store in London](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3239/2777192203_b6792b5e84.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/2777192203/ "Nike 1948 Popup Store in London by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

And inside there’s limited edition shoes and clothes made by Nike all in a swanky sport-themed interior. It’s a pretty nice thing.

[![Nike 1948 Popup Store in London](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3245/2777192147_f31c0fe49f.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/2777192147/ "Nike 1948 Popup Store in London by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

It’s here, in case you want directions…
