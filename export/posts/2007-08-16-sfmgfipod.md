[This made me LOL. It’s a music blog post](http://www.missingtoof.com/v2/2007/08/15/sfmgfipod-4/). Normally the guy posts up decent music, but he’s got a thread called [SFMGFiPod](http://www.missingtoof.com/tag/sfmygfipod/) (which stands for ‘shit from my girlfriend’s iPod’). He’s basically posting up the stuff that his girlfriend is listening to. By seeing the two playlists I started to almost imagine what their relationship is like based on the musical compatibility or otherwise of their two playlists.

You could do something quite funny with an online MP3 version of [Mr and Mrs](http://www.ukgameshows.com/page/index.php/Mr_and_Mrs).
