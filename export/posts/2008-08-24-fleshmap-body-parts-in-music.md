This site’s got some brilliant visualisations of how often [various body parts are mentioned in different genres of music.](http://www.fleshmap.com/listen/music.html)

![Fleshmap: Listen: Music](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080824-gm84iy17asubhrcamr816cirxh.jpg?w=660)

Once you’re there you can drill down into the various genres and see what’s occurring.

![Fleshmap: Listen: Hip Hop](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080824-cdp73hfgsb2m7p448hr9spia94.jpg?w=660)  
![Fleshmap: Listen: Electronica](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080824-1iaiubiaaf1chya8fixmaesef1.jpg?w=660)

Which is nice. But the best bit is that there’s a great feature where you can [create a Rebus by copying and pasting bits of text into the box](http://www.fleshmap.com/listen/rebus_interactive.html) and the application pulls out the mentions of body parts and turns them into graphics.

I thought I’d give it a go, so I turned to one of my favourite artists – [Craig David](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G1bTqfbXnZs). And used his lyrics to create a couple of rebuses for myself. You can see the diversity of emotions that Craig deals with in his songs below.

From the romantic ‘face based’ soulful R&B of Rendezvous:

![Fleshmap: Listen: Interactive Body Rebus](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080824-qt458y4q8434meiujqrwfnmsu.jpg?w=660)

To the much more posterior focused “Booty Man”:

![Fleshmap: Listen: Interactive Body Rebus](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080824-gqxug368n4gi99mrbs5nijajc2.jpg?w=660)

Simple but effective.
