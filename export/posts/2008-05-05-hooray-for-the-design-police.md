I’ve been banging on to a couple of designers about how they need to [start up something like this](http://www.design-police.org/). They need to hunt down and bring to justice all of the horrible design that keeps creeping into some of our documents. Now someone’s done all the hard work. [All we need to do is print out the stickers](http://www.design-police.org/).

![](/wp-content/uploads/2008/05/design_police1.gif "design_police1")

And it’s not just design crimes. There’s also stickers to highlight conceptual and stylistic crimes too…

![](/wp-content/uploads/2008/05/design_police2.gif "design_police2")
