[![box.net screengrab](/wp-content/uploads/2006/05/box_net.jpg)](http://www.box.net "box.net screengrab")I was looking for a free online storage thing for Sophie (so she could share stuff between home and work). There’s loads and loads of them around right now. But I wanted something with plenty of storage space and that’s easy to use. After a fair bit of digging around I unearthed [Box.net](http://www.box.net/): not only has it got 1Gb of free storage, but it’s got a really nice interface too (yeah yeah, it’s all rounded corners and there’s a load of 2.0 cliches in there, but the drag and drop bits work great and it’s visually very pleasant).

Oh, and it’s got RSS built in, and you can share things privately and publicly. And lots of other stuff too. And and just to reiterate, it’s free.
