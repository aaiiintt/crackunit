I went to the Goldsmiths Design Graduate show on Brick Lane yesterday lunchtime. It was pretty good. Well there were 2 or 3 things that I really liked. You can get to the website here: [http://pirateutopias.com](http://pirateutopias.com)

One guy in particular stood out.

[![Liam Healy's Page From Goldsmiths Show](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3349/3588210845_6f8847bb00.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3588210845/ "Liam Healy's Page From Goldsmiths Show by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Liam Healy has done a bunch of projects that I really liked. From the brochure he says:

[![Liam Healy's Manifesto](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2433/3588210979_e147fea6d6.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3588210979/ "Liam Healy's Manifesto by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Basically he’s the kid that when parents said “don’t do that”, he’d say “why?”. They’d say “just because”. Then he’d say “but why?”. And I don’t think he’s ever given up.

My favourite 2 of his machines are an experiment that jams a knife into a toaster to see if it can electrocute you – an early prototype here:

[![Machine](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3613/3404308476_d6a8b7edf3.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/liam-h/3404308476/ "Machine by liam_healy, on Flickr")

And a powertool that flicks a light switch on-and-off really really fast to see if it’s a bad thing. Here’s what happened when he took the ‘flickerer’ to his dad (the source of the original ‘myth’). Not only is it a great project, but the video’s bloody great too, apart from the volume levels :-(

<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/4542496" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

And can eating Mentos and drinking Diet Coke at the same time kill you? I love the bit halfway through where one ‘pops back out’ – it’s LOLfunny.

<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/3978698" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Check his [blog](http://liam-h.com/blog/) or [Vimeo channel](http://vimeo.com/user1319289) for more work – should you eat with a knife? Can you cook an egg with a mobile phone? Will a hairdryer in a bath kill you? Should you tap the top of a shaken up can?

It’s a bit like Junior Mythbusters, only there’s something more real and humble about it. I’m a fan.
