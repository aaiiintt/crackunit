![kettle chips party pack](/wp-content/uploads/2006/11/party-packS-4.jpg)I’m a bit of crisp junkie as I’ve said before (even post the [drinking oil advert that put me off a bit](/2006/10/04/mmmmm-crisps/)).

So I’m a bit biased, but I like this thought. Buy this [Kettle Chips party box](http://market.kettlebrand.com/roi/617/HAPPYHOUR/Peoples-Choice-Passport-to-Flavor-Party-Pack-PCHHMP108.htm) online for $19.99 which contains:

-   Five 5 oz. bags of People’s Choice chips, one of each flavor
-   “A Taste of Putumayo: Music for Every Palate” world music sampler CD
-   trivia postcards
-   voting ballots
-   food pairing ideas, drink recipes and a premium chip clip

Then you can go online at [http://www.passporttoflavor.com](http://www.passporttoflavor.com) and vote for your favourite of the 5 limited edition flavours with a chance to win a decent prize.

It’s maybe not everyone’s cup of tea, but in terms of getting crisp lovers to get involved with new product development I quite liked it.
