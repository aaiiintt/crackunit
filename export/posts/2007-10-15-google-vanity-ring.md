![Google Ring](/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/google_ring.jpg)

Too good. An project that looks at jewelery and status in the digital age. The creator [Markus Kison says](http://www.digital.udk-berlin.de/en/projects/summer07/haupt/bodytech/googlering.html):

> Update of the ring as a status symbol. It shows the number of Google hits you get, when you search for the name of the person who wears it.

Basically it’s a ring that shows how popular the wearer is based on Google hits. It’s fully syncable so it can have all the latest scores.

Read more about it [here](http://www.digital.udk-berlin.de/en/projects/summer07/haupt/bodytech/googlering.html) – there’s also a [video that shows the prototype working](http://www.markuskison.de/ring/VanityRing-Video.html).

Apparently he’s looking for someone to help take it into production…
