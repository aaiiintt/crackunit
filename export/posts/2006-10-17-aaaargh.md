[![Ms Dewey](/wp-content/uploads/2006/10/Picture%2021.jpg)](http://www.msdewey.com/)Could this be the most pointless thing ever? Probably not. But it’s a massive folly of some description.  
Someone has spent a whole heap of money creating a video based ‘human face’ to a search engine. Type in your search and [Ms Dewey](http://www.msdewey.com/) makes pointless and irrelevant quips. Then you get a badly presented list of search returns.

It’s got to be a marketing campaign for something. No one else would waste money in such a stupid fashion ;-)
