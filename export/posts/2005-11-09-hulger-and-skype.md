[![hulger phone](/wp-content/hulger.jpg)](http://www.hulger.com/)Nik, one of my co-partners at [Poke](http://www.pokelondon.com/) has, in my opinion, finally nailed a proper useful-use for his [Hulger Phones](http://www.hulger.com/). Although an interesting design statement and a humerous novelty when attached to a mobile, I don’t think anyone would really use them in the longer term. But, they sure beat a crappy headset for VOIP. So a partnership with Skype seems like a winner to me!
