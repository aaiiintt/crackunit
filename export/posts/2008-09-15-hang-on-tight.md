Artist Kenny Irwin posts under the name [perfectlymadebirds on Flickr](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kennyirwin/). His photos and their descriptions are totally amazing. I found myself getting sucked into his weird universe for more time than is strictly acceptable for someone on the right side of the mentalist line.

I’m not going to say any more, I’ll only spoil it. I’ll just suggest a couple of ‘drop-in points’…

-   [Pakistani Starfleet Explorers](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kennyirwin/sets/72157604476547192/)
-   [\*\*\*The dOvestar Chronicles\*\*\*](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kennyirwin/sets/72157600219969607/)

And do make sure you read the descriptions, it really helps to contextualise things.

Once you get into Kenny’s art, like I have, you’ll want to head over to [www.redbubble.com](/wp-admin/www.redbubble.com/people/dovetastic) to buy some of his artworks as prints.

p.s. He also has a great collection of eyes (including these glass Cat and Goat eyes).
