[![CMO](/wp-content/uploads/2007/01/cmo.gif)](http://fallontrendpoint.blogspot.com/2007/01/ad-agency-deathwatch.html)Following the [BBH Lynx win post](/2007/01/11/bbh-wins-lynx-digital-account/) I thought [this research into the attitude of CMOs](http://www.sapient.com/about+us/pressrelease/?postingid=%2fabout%2bus%2fpressrelease%2fCMOs%2bLook%2bBeyond%2bTraditional%2bAgencies%2bfor%2bInnovation%2bin%2bMarketing.htm)(commissioned by [Sapient](http://www.sapient.com)) reported some interesting stuff. LIke the fact that 68% of CMOs prefer to work with multiple agencies, to derive the benefits of specialisation, and that 49% of survey respondents believe that traditional advertising firms have difficulty thinking beyond traditional print and TV media models. But then again it was commissioned by a digital agency ;-)
