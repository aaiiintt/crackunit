In under 24 hours I’ve noticed these 2 ads for what I suppose are Google’s nearest competitors in a bunch of things.

First this Yahoo! ad.

[![IMG\_0546](/wp-content/rescued/farm5.static.flickr.com/4085/4993603469_6fffda659e_z.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4993603469/ "IMG_0546 by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Now I’m not crazy about the chick with the Social Media Tattoos. I’ve seen her around a bit and I find her a little bit, um, contrived. Especially when you put her outdoors in the middle of Tattoo-town (Portland, OR).

But then as I walked closer to the ad I noticed…

[![IMG\_0549](/wp-content/rescued/farm5.static.flickr.com/4084/4993603879_9959419009_z.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4993603879/ "IMG_0549 by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Holy shit, it’s hand painted!

[![IMG\_0551](/wp-content/rescued/farm5.static.flickr.com/4103/4994210542_844fd513ee_z.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4994210542/ "IMG_0551 by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

At the very least this deserves an A for effort. Even if the ad is a bit horrible.

Then, this morning, I noticed this Microsoft Bing ad for the Portland Foodcarts Map.

[![IMG\_0555](/wp-content/rescued/farm5.static.flickr.com/4113/4993620617_2e7b1c5715.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/4993620617/ "IMG_0555 by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

You can view the map here: [http://www.bing.com/foodcarts](http://www.bing.com/foodcarts). The site takes a while to load and it’s not the best experience ever (and it only works in some browsers – not Chrome). But they have gone and made a theoretically useful map of Portland Food Carts. So again, A for effort.

It’s good to see these guys making an effort.
