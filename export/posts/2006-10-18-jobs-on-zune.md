From Fast Company: [Steve Jobs on the iPod .Vs Zune](http://blog.fastcompany.com/archives/2006/10/17/steve_jobs_on_the_ipod_vs_zune.html) . Steve Jobs blasts the ability to ‘send’ music to other people via Microsoft’s new Zune player (apparantly it takes a long time and they can only play the DRM enabled track 3 times):

> You’re much better off to take one of your earbuds out and put it in  
> her ear. Then you’re connected with about two feet of headphone cable

I can see the Microsoft ads now: ***Zune – sharing music without the earwax!***
