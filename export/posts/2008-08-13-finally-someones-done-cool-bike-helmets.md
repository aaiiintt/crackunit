Big up the Danish company [Yakkay](http://www.yakkay.com/uk/forside.html). I’m so glad that someone’s made cycling helmets that don’t look like some piss-poor [Tron](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tron_\(film\)) rip-off or a bit of Super Mario fancy dress. It’s been on my list of things to sort out in the world for a while.

With the [Yakkay system](http://www.yakkay.com/uk/forside.html) you buy the base helmet, and then you can choose nice covers for them…

![YAKKAY - brainwear for smart people. Bicycle Helmet, cover, Izmir, Dublin, Paris, Tokyo.](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080813-x1ijibrb5hma96jgqs6hfx6fr6.jpg?w=660)

I’ve got a couple of reservations though…

They might just look like really big comedy “It’s A Knockout” hats. How do I know this model hasn’t got a massive head that makes the helmet look OK?

![Min sang](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080813-c9tjiyxasiqk8ns88a6ppwydr2.jpg?w=660)

And also I’m not sure that this looks like the most robust and safest type of helmet in the world. I wouldn’t trust myself to get the pads in the right places to save my life…

![Min sang](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080813-kq7wcia6s3u7bbw32taf4u8cfe.jpg?w=660)
