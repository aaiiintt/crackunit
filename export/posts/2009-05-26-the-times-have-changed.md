I went to a WW2 day in Bletchley with Sophie, my Gran, my Mum and my Auntie. It was a nice day.

I couldn’t help but notice the similarities and differences between that and other similar holiday weekends that I’ve had over the years. And those Twitter messages streaming in from the [Detroit Electronic Music Festival](http://www.demf.com/) ([#demf](http://search.twitter.com/search?q=%23demf)) weren’t helping much.

The queue for the party stretched round the block:

[![Bletchley Park WW2 Day](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3402/3566689220_a0215fe8c9.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3566689220/ "Bletchley Park WW2 Day by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Security was tight, but just right:

[![Bletchley Park WW2 Day - Home Guard](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2427/3565875949_7c74fbc93a.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3565875949/ "Bletchley Park WW2 Day - Home Guard by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

But the long arm of the law was never far away:

[![Bletchley Park WW2 Day - The Fuzz](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3578/3565875711_93d2f23892.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3565875711/ "Bletchley Park WW2 Day - The Fuzz by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

As usual the promoter was looking pretty pleased with himself:

[![Bletchley Park WW2 Day - The Business](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3409/3566688482_997ddea66a.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3566688482/ "Bletchley Park WW2 Day - The Business by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

People were totally loving it:

[![Lindyhop Spinner](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2472/3565875261_c42bee3b18.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3565875261/ "Lindyhop Spinner by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

And the dancefloor was totally going off:

[![Lindyhop Running Man](/wp-content/rescued/farm3.static.flickr.com/2427/3565874647_b53bf6f8cb.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3565874647/ "Lindyhop Running Man by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

The DJ totally rocked it:

[![Come my selector](/wp-content/rescued/farm4.static.flickr.com/3610/3566687252_86309c14ae.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/iaintait/3566687252/ "Come my selector by Iain Tait, on Flickr")

Like I said. Same same but very different ;-)
