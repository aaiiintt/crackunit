I was chatting to [Jordy van den Nieuwendijk](http://jordyvandennieuwendijk.nl/) at [Here](http://here-london.com/) on Friday. And he mentioned that he’d worked with a guy at W+K NYC who was responsible for [Waiting in Line 3D](http://www.waitinginline3d.com/).

WIL3D is one of my favorite things of all time, and having the warped mind behind it working for the same company is beyond thrilling!

<iframe loading="lazy" src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/82807354" width="660" height="371" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="" mozallowfullscreen="" allowfullscreen=""></iframe>

So like a proper fanboy I sent [Rajeev](http://www.basuranewyork.com/) a message of love and admiration. Rajeev send a nice message back, and pointed me at a recent project called [Nice2HackYou](http://www.nice2hackyou.com/) which is also for a band, and also mind-bendingly awesome.

I installed the [Chrome Extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/nice-2-hack-you-by-big-da/pllnecbdobiliofejcobkfafgnjdkdej?hl=en) and held my breath. Thankfully I installed it on a ‘clean’ machine. So I’ve got very little Fox News or other disgusting items in my browser history…
