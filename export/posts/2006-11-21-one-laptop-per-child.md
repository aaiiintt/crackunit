> The MIT Media Lab has launched a new research initiative to develop a

And the [machine itself is shaping up to be really interesting](http://wiki.laptop.org/go/Hardware_specification). I’m pretty fond of the design too. It may look a bit like a toy, but when you read the specs it’s far from it.
