![Picture 10](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/Picture-10-300x58.png "Picture 10")

I’ve not been a huge fan of [Spotify](http://www.spotify.com). I can’t put my finger on why not. I love the idea. I quite like the interface and things. I’ve even come to terms with Jonathan and his ads.

I think that my problem is that I can’t find the music I want. Faced with an empty search box (when I know the results are going to be quite mainstream) my mind just goes blank. And I end up listening to things that I hate, just because they pop into my head quickly.

But a couple of minutes round [sharemyplaylists.com](http://www.sharemyplaylists.com) and I’ve found:

-   The [most sampled breakbeats ever](http://www.sharemyplaylists.com/the-most-sampled-breakbeats/) – a great collection of funk, soul and more.
-   A [history of Detroit techno](http://www.sharemyplaylists.com/techno-the-undergound-sound-of-detroit/)
-   A bunch of [mod scooter classics](http://www.sharemyplaylists.com/scootering-classics/)
-   Celebrations of some of the most influential indie labels, [like XL Recordings](http://www.sharemyplaylists.com/independents-day-09-xl-recordings/)
-   And of course some [whomping trance classics](http://www.sharemyplaylists.com/trance-classics/)

I’m hoping that this might open up the floodgates to the world of Spotify for me.
