Just realised I posted a [dumb comment over on Ben’s blog](http://noisydecentgraphics.typepad.com/design/2008/10/wouldnt-it-be-b.html#comments). He’d [blogged about how we should re-use our Amazon boxes and how it’d be better than recycling](http://noisydecentgraphics.typepad.com/design/2008/10/wouldnt-it-be-b.html).
