Here’s [a link to a site that posts lots of great mixes of the greatest music in the world](http://www.allez-allez.co.uk). If I was younger and lived in London town I go to all these guys’ parties and be like their total groupie. They’ve even got a mega Kompakt party this weekend.

So many great mixes. I’m listening to a [phenomenal Euan Pearson one](http://www.allez-allez.co.uk/2007/12/ewan-pearson.html) on my iPod right now.

Subscribe to their podcast and all the juicy goodness will run into your earholes with a minimum of effort. I did. And it made me a more fulfilled person. Really.
