My name is Iain. I’ve only done Sudoku once. But I think I sort of enjoyed it…

Commuting has something to be said for it. You get a block of time that you can use to do stuff. I think I could learn something from giving myself blocks of time to concentrate on something. If you put your mind to it, you can even put numbers in boxes and make them add up to 9. Imagine that!
