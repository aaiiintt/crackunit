[Andy](http://nowincolour.blogspot.com/) our latest new [Poke](http://www.pokelondon.com) chap alerted me to the awesomeness of [Compfight.com](http://www.compfight.com). It’s a Flickr search tool that allows you to find and view lots of Flickr images quickly with some key filters (like Creative Commons licensing). It’s a top way of finding images for comps, research and inspiration. Nice one.

I recommend you subscribe to [Andy’s blog](http://nowincolour.blogspot.com/), it’s really rather good.
