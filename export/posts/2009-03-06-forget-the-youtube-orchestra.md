A while ago I posted about [making a YouTube band](/2008/08/13/making-a-youtube-band/) by mashing together random clips of people playing together and how sometimes it sounded pretty sweet.

Well this guy Kutiman has gone one better and nailed a whole bunch of really rich, dense downtempo tracks using snippets of videos from YouTube. They are absolutely incredible.
