Check out [VectorMagic](http://vectormagic.stanford.edu/) it’s a fantastic online vectorization tool (I’m not sure why but I like vectorization better with a z than an s – I’m sure I’m not allowed to choose willy nilly like that, but this time I’m just going to).

Basically what it does is take a photo, like so:

![Photo](/wp-content/uploads/2007/11/photo-5.jpg)

And vectorizes is, thusly:

![Vector](/wp-content/uploads/2007/11/photo_5_vectorized.jpg)

You can set the levels and things online and then download the file to use as you want. Very slick online app.
