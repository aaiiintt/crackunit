[Mr Oizo](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Oizo) is probably best known for the whole Flat Eric thing. Which apparently as well as soundtracking he also directed, which was new news to me. Since then he’s made lots of banging electronic music. Most of which is just a little too far out for me.

I just came across this mini documentary about the making of his new album – Lamb’s Anger. It confirms that he’s a total nutter. In a good way.
