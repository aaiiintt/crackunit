I’m on my way to LA to take part in the [Webby Connect Conference](http://www.webbyawards.com/webbyconnect/) and I’m very much looking forward to it. If you’re in the area or going to the conference drop me a line…

Unfortunately, this means that I won’t be able to go to the Glug event on Thursday Night. I’ve never actually been to one yet, and it’s their 7th time. But I like the guys who run it, and I like their strapline: “More Notworking then Networking”.

Or on [Facebook](http://www.facebook.com/group.php?gid=8353864836). And while you’re there check out [their Xmas challenge](http://gluglondon.wordpress.com/2008/10/16/coming-soon-glug-xmas-bash/) – there’s some nice prizes to be won…
