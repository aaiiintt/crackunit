# Asset order, 11-09 (Stage 1 look boards, look C · Raytraced)

The Raytraced look (PLAN.md §1.2 C, refs 12 and 11) needs two rendered assets
that the archive cannot supply and that must not be faked in CSS. The three
Raytraced stills (`otd/lookdev/raytraced/1.html` to `3.html`) are written
against the delivery paths below and render as soon as the files exist:

```bash
node otd/lookdev/render.mjs raytraced && open otd/lookdev/out/contact.png
```

Where they go: item 1 is the whole ground of all three Raytraced stills; item 2
sits on the floor at the right of still 1, about 30% of frame width. Still 2
maps a Post-it Note Waterfall frame onto a plane floating in the room (CSS 3D
transform on the frame, soft shadow drawn under it); still 3 floats a Win95-
style list of the nine titles at 3×. Both of those only need item 1.

## An alternative, if you would rather not hand-make them

POV-Ray is the literal 1996 tool and renders this scene in seconds. If you say
"povray", I will `brew install povray` (a machine dependency, not a repo one),
write the `.pov` scene into `otd/lookdev/assets/room-1996.pov`, render both
items at the sizes above, and commit the scene file so the room is reproducible.
Nano Banana Pro or Midjourney would also do item 1 in one shot with the
description in the table; item 2 is easier to raytrace than to prompt.
